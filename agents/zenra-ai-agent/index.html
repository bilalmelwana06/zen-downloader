What is Zenra AI Agent?

A ready-made AI chat agent for your business. It answers customer questions on your website or on a chat link you share, day and night. It saves every lead and passes the chat to your team when a person is needed.

Do I need a website?

No. After you go live you get a chat link and a QR code. Put the link in your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook bio, your WhatsApp status and your Google Business profile, and print the QR code on menus, flyers and your counter.

Which website platforms does it work with?

It works with all of them, including WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Blogger, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Google Sites and any HTML website. You paste one line of code and the chat bubble appears on every page. The app shows the exact steps for each platform.

Does my computer need to stay on?

No. Once it's live, the agent runs in the cloud on free Cloudflare hosting and answers customers all day and night. Open the app whenever you like to see new chats and leads. They come in by themselves.

How do I train it?

Add your business info, let it read your website, upload files (PDF, Word, CSV or text) or paste text. Short facts like prices, opening hours and delivery go into Key facts, which it keeps in mind for every answer. If an answer isn't right, you teach it a better one and it's fixed for every customer.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language your customer writes in, including English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Hindi and Arabic.

What happens when a customer wants a real person?

The agent passes the chat to your team and shows WhatsApp and email buttons to the customer. You can get a phone alert the moment it happens.

Can it give my prices?

Yes, if you want it to. In Rules you choose whether it may share prices. It only quotes prices from your own information and never makes up a number.

Are there monthly costs for AI or hosting?

It can run at no cost, because it works with free AI keys (the app shows you how to get one in a minute) and free Cloudflare hosting. You can add paid AI keys any time for even faster replies.

Which computer do I need?

The Zenra AI Agent app, where you set up, train and see your chats, runs on Windows 10 and 11. Your customers can chat from any phone, tablet or computer.

How do I get Zenra AI Agent?

Tap Get Zenra AI Agent on this page or email us at support@zenraai.com. We'll answer your questions and help you get it running on your website.

For bookings, order tracking, internal tools or work across several apps, our team builds custom AI agents with approvals for anything important.

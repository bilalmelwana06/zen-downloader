Zenra AI Viral ClipsAvailable now

Turn long videos into viral clips, automatically.

Zenra AI Viral Clips is a Windows app that cuts long videos into short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. Add a podcast, interview, vlog or sports video. The AI finds the strong moments, keeps the speaker or the action in frame, adds word-by-word captions and a title, and saves every clip in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9, up to 4K.

A professional editor inside your PC

Everything an editor does to make a clip work, done for you, clip after clip.

Finds every viral moment

The AI reads your whole video like an editor (a strong hook, a complete story, a payoff) and makes a clip of each moment. Longer videos give more clips, and your best clip is always #01.

Clean starts and endings

Clips start with the question and end when the answer is finished, never on a cut-off word. Intros, sponsor reads and “thanks for watching” outros are left out.

Follows whoever is talking

Speaker detection watches lips and listens to voices, so the frame stays on the person talking and cuts the moment someone else speaks, like a real editor.

Split screen for conversations

When two people go back and forth, you see both: one on top, one below. Panels with three or four people stay lively instead of one long monologue.

Follows the action

In sports and vlogs the whole person stays in view. After a shot the frame moves with the ball when it can be seen, so the clip shows where it went. What a vlogger holds up stays in frame too.

Every clip size

9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. 4:5 and 1:1 for feed posts. 16:9 for YouTube, X and LinkedIn. Choose the size before you make the clips and the framing fits it.

Scorecards stay on the clip

Golf scorecards and other score graphics are kept on screen when the picture is cropped, and they never cover a face.

Videos without talking

Golf ASMR, silent vlogs and music videos get clips built around the action: the sound of the hit and the camera cuts. No made-up captions.

Word-by-word captions

Each word lights up as it's spoken. Eight styles, words always in the right order, and no “um” or “uh”.

Titles, hashtags and a hook title

Every clip gets a title and hashtags. Files are named Title - 01.mp4, and one click copies the title for posting. Turn on the hook title and the title also shows at the top of the clip, in one of three looks.

Up to 4K quality

Original, 4K, 2K or 1080p. Intel, NVIDIA and AMD graphics speed up the final videos automatically.

Your logo on every clip

Add your logo once and it sits small and soft in the top-left corner of every clip, like a TV channel logo. No Zenra watermark.

Same clips, or new ones

Run a video again and get the same clips instantly. That is handy for changing captions, logo, size or quality. New clips finds only moments you haven't made yet.

13 languages plus auto detect

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi and Indonesian.

Free AI built in, more if you want

Runs on a free Groq key. You can add another free key (Gemini, Cerebras, Mistral or OpenRouter free models) or a paid one (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok or OpenRouter).

Always the right framing

Your video is wide and a short is tall. Shot by shot, the app decides what belongs in the frame.

Follows the speaker

The frame stays on whoever is talking and cuts instantly when someone else speaks.

Split screen

A quick back-and-forth shows both people (one on top, one below) for that part only.

The whole person

Vlogs, walk-and-talks and sports keep the person head to toe. The frame moves with them, and with the ball after a shot.

The whole picture

A title card, a leaderboard or two people far apart is shown whole on a soft background, so nothing you need to read or see is cut.

On Auto (smart) the app picks the framing for every shot. You can also choose Follow speaker, Split screen or Full frame in Settings. A face is never cut by the frame, the hook title or a scorecard.

One video, every clip size

Choose the clip size on the CREATE tab before you press MAKE VIRAL CLIPS. The framing, the captions and the title are fitted to the size you pick.

Shorts, Reels and TikTok

The tall size for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Stories.

Instagram and Facebook feed

Takes more of the screen in the feed than a square post.

Square posts

Works in every feed, and fits two speakers side by side.

YouTube, X and LinkedIn

The wide size, for highlight clips on YouTube, X and LinkedIn.

Want the same moments in another size? Run the video again with a different size: the app remembers the clips it chose, so there is no AI wait.

Captions that keep people watching

Every word lights up the moment it's spoken. Pick one of eight styles, or turn captions off.

A caption never mixes two speakers, and the words always appear in the order they're said.

How it works

Four steps from a long video to clips you can post.

  1. Add your video

    Drag it onto the app or click BROWSE. Long videos are welcome: the longer the video, the more clips you get.

  2. Pick your style

    Choose the clip size (9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9), the quality up to 4K, the clip length from 15 seconds to 3 minutes, the caption style and your logo.

  3. Make viral clips

    Press MAKE VIRAL CLIPS. The AI finds the moments and edits each clip. They appear one by one.

  4. Post them

    Every clip has PLAY, COPY TITLE and SHOW IN FOLDER. Start with #01. It's your best clip.

Complete guide

Everything you need, from installing the app to adding another AI key.

Install the app
  1. Click Download at the top of this page. You get ZenraAIViralClips.exe.
  2. Open it and click Next until Finish. No admin password is needed. Tick Create a desktop shortcut if you like.
  3. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. New apps without a paid certificate show this once.
  4. Open Zenra AI Viral Clips. Your 30-day free trial starts by itself (internet is needed the first time).
Get your free Groq key (2 minutes)

Groq is a free AI service. Its key lets the app write the words and run the AI editor, which gives the best results at no cost.

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign in with Google or email.
  2. Click Create API Key, type any name and copy the key. It starts with gsk_.
  3. In the app, open SETTINGS → GROQ AI KEY, paste the key and press SAVE KEY.
  4. Press TEST KEY. You should see Key works.

Your key is saved encrypted on your PC. Without a key the app still works offline, only slower and with simpler titles.

Make your first clips
  1. On the CREATE tab, drag your video onto the window or click BROWSE (Ctrl + O). MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WEBM and most other video files work.
  2. Quality: Original keeps your video's quality (a 4K video gives 4K clips). 4K, 2K and 1080p are there too.
  3. Clip length: 15 sec, 30 sec, 45 sec, 1 min, 2 min or 3 min.
  4. Captions: Viral Yellow, Green Pop, Zenra Cyan, Hormozi, Clean White, Karaoke, TikTok, Punch Words or Off. Logo: click Add logo and choose a PNG.
  5. Clip size: 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 4:5 for the Instagram and Facebook feed, 1:1 for a square post or 16:9 for YouTube, X and LinkedIn.
  6. Hook title on the clip: Off, Big Title, Post Card or Black Box. It shows the clip's title at the top, like the header of a viral reel.
  7. Press MAKE VIRAL CLIPS. Clips appear one by one with their title and viral score. STOP keeps every clip already made.
Find and post your clips
  • Every clip card has PLAY, COPY TITLE (title and hashtags) and SHOW IN FOLDER.
  • Clips are saved in Downloads\Zenra AI Viral Clips\ in a folder named after your video, as Title - 01.mp4, Title - 02.mp4 and so on. 01 is the best clip.
  • The MY CLIPS tab keeps every video you've clipped, with OPEN FOLDER, COPY ALL TITLES and VIEW CLIPS.
Same clips again, or new ones
  • The same video with the same clip length gives the same clips again, with no waiting for the AI. Use it to change the captions, logo, clip size or quality.
  • After a run, press NEW CLIPS to get only moments you haven't made yet, never a repeat. When every good moment is used, the app tells you.
Settings explained
  • Framing: Auto (smart), Follow speaker, Split screen or Full frame (no crop).
  • Number of clips: Auto (every viral moment), or up to 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 50.
  • Video language: Auto detect, or choose one of 13 languages.
  • Offline speech model: used when there's no internet. Fast, Better or Best (slow).
  • Scorecards and score graphics: On keeps a golf scorecard or score graphic on the clip when the picture is cropped.
  • Save clips to: any folder you like.
Add another AI key, free or paid (optional)

The free Groq key is enough for most people. Settings has two more cards under it, one for free keys and one for paid keys. Only one key runs the AI editor at a time: choose a company in one card and the other card switches to Off.

  • More free AI keys: Google Gemini, Cerebras, Mistral or the free models on OpenRouter. No bank card is needed. Use one when the free Groq limit is reached, or to try another AI. A free key allows a set amount of work per minute, so a long video can take a little longer.
  • Paid AI keys: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, xAI Grok or OpenRouter. The AI reads the whole video at once, so there is no free-limit waiting and the strongest models pick the clips. You pay the AI company directly, about 1 to 5 US cents per one-hour video.
  1. In the app, open SETTINGS and go to MORE FREE AI KEYS or PAID AI KEYS.
  2. Choose the AI company, then click Get a key to open its key page.
  3. Paste your key and press SAVE KEY. It is tested automatically.
  4. Leave the AI model on Auto (recommended).

The words (speech-to-text) still come from free Groq or the offline model. If your key runs out of credit or reaches its limit, the app switches back to Groq by itself, so your job never stops. Every key is saved encrypted on your PC.

Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready you see a popup. One click, and it installs and reopens. You can also check at any time in SETTINGS → CHECK FOR UPDATES.

Fix common problems
  • “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway.
  • “Groq key not accepted”: make a new key at console.groq.com/keys, paste it, press SAVE KEY, then TEST KEY.
  • Clips are slow: choose 1080p quality, or set the offline speech model to Fast.
  • Free Groq limit reached: the app moves to another model by itself. For many long videos a day, add a second free key or a paid key in Settings.
  • Something went wrong: press COPY DETAILS in the message and send it to us on WhatsApp. SETTINGS → OPEN LOGS (FOR SUPPORT) opens the folder with the app's log file. It records what the app did, never your keys, and helps us find a problem fast.

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What is Zenra AI Viral Clips?

Zenra AI Viral Clips is a Windows app from Zenra AI that turns long videos into short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and feed posts. The AI picks the strong moments, frames the speaker or the action, adds word-by-word captions and a title, and saves every clip on your PC, up to 4K.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new PC gets a 30-day free trial with every feature. No card and no sign-up.

What do I need to use it?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC and an internet connection. For the best results, add a free Groq key. It takes about two minutes (see the complete guide). A Mac version is coming soon.It also runs on Mac.

Which videos work best?

Podcasts, interviews, vlogs, speeches, stand-up comedy, streams, webinars, classes, documentaries and sports such as golf. Videos without talking work too: the clips are built around the action. MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WEBM and most other video files work, up to 4K.

Which clip sizes can it make?

Four. 9:16 for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, 4:5 for the Instagram and Facebook feed, 1:1 for square posts, and 16:9 for YouTube, X and LinkedIn. You choose the size before making the clips and the framing fits it.

Does it work for sports and golf videos?

Yes. The whole player stays in the frame, and after a shot the frame moves with the ball when it can be seen. Golf scorecards and score graphics stay on the clip without covering a face. A video with no talking gets clips built around the action, with no made-up captions.

How many clips will I get?

On Auto you get a clip of every strong moment, so longer videos give more clips. A one-hour podcast gives about 30 clips of 15 seconds, or about 10 clips of 3 minutes. You can also limit it to 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 50.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Your video stays on your PC. Finding faces, framing, captions and rendering all happen on your computer. With a Groq key, the audio is sent to Groq to write the words, and the text is read by the AI editor (Groq, or another AI key if you added one). Without a key, nothing from your video leaves your PC.

Do I have to pay for the AI?

No. The app runs on a free Groq key. You can also add another free key (Google Gemini, Cerebras, Mistral or the free models on OpenRouter) or a paid key (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok or OpenRouter). One key runs the AI editor at a time, and if it stops working the app goes back to Groq by itself.

Do the clips have a watermark?

No. There is no Zenra watermark. Only your own logo appears, if you add one.

Which languages are supported?

Auto detect, plus English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi and Indonesian.

How do I buy a plan?

In the app, click UPGRADE, choose a plan and tap BUY WITH WHATSAPP. Tell us your country and we'll share the payment options available to you. After payment you receive a license key. Paste it in the app and press ACTIVATE.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We share the options available in your country on WhatsApp, for example bank transfer, mobile wallets or Binance. Prices are in US dollars and the same in every country.

Can I use one key on two PCs?

Each key works on one PC. If you change your computer, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help.

Does it work without internet?

The AI and the plan check need internet. After a check, the app keeps working for up to 7 days offline, using the offline speech model and simpler titles.

How do I get new versions?

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready you see a popup. One click and it installs and reopens.

Windows shows a blue warning screen. Is it safe?

Yes. New apps without a code-signing certificate can show a SmartScreen warning. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Which websites are supported?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), Snapchat and many other video websites.

Can I download a whole playlist, channel or account?

Yes. Paste the playlist, channel or account link and the app saves every video into its own folder - several at a time.

Which qualities can I choose?

4K, 1440p, 1080p or MP3 audio - or Best quality, which picks the highest quality the video offers.

Will the videos play on my phone and TV?

Yes. With MP4 mode on (it's on by default), videos are saved as standard MP4 files (H.264 video with AAC sound), which phones, tablets, smart TVs and every media player can play. If a site sends another format, the app converts it automatically.

What if a big download stops halfway?

Press Retry. The app skips the videos that are already saved and continues with the rest - a channel download that stopped at video 50 continues from video 51.

Will I get duplicate files?

No. Videos that are already in your folder are recognized and skipped, and two different videos with the same title are saved side by side.

How do I get new versions?

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready you see a banner - one click and it installs and reopens. The download engine also stays up to date automatically.

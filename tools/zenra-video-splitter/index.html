Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zenra Video Splitter free?

Yes. Zenra Video Splitter is free — no watermark, no sign-up and no upload.

Does it lose video quality?

No. In most cases the clips are copied without re-encoding, so they keep 100% of the original quality. Only when the video's structure needs it are a few frames at a cut re-encoded, at very high quality.

Will my clips fit WhatsApp Status?

Yes. When your clips are close to a 60 s or 90 s limit (or 3 minutes for Shorts and Reels), the app makes sure no clip goes over it.

Which video formats work?

MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WEBM and most other video files.

Does it work on my computer?

Zenra Video Splitter works on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).

How do I get new versions?

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready you see a popup — one click and it installs and reopens.

Windows shows a blue warning screen. Is it safe?

Yes. New apps without a code-signing certificate can show a SmartScreen warning. Click More info, then Run anyway.

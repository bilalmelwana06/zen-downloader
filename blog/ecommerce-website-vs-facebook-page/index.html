E-commerce Website vs Facebook Page: Which Is Better for Selling Online?

By Bilal Melwana • 25 September 2026

Many sellers start on a Facebook or Instagram page — and that is a great way to begin. But as orders grow, a page alone starts to feel limiting. Here is an honest comparison to help you decide.

Selling through a Facebook page

  • Pros: free to start, your audience is already there, easy to share products.
  • Cons: orders get lost in messages, no proper product catalogue, and you depend on one platform's reach.

Selling through your own e-commerce website

  • Pros: a professional catalogue, organised orders, a brand that customers trust, and pages that can rank on Google.
  • Cons: needs setup, and someone has to keep it updated.

The best answer: use both

Use Facebook and Instagram to reach customers, and your website to convert them. Your social posts and ads send people to product pages where ordering is simple and organised.

When is it time to build a store?

  • You spend hours every day answering the same product questions.
  • Orders are getting mixed up in chats.
  • You want customers to find you on Google.

How to download videos on a PC in HD and 4K (2026 guide)

A step-by-step guide to saving videos, playlists and audio on Windows in the quality you want — and how to do it responsibly.

Updated October 21, 20263 min readZenra AI team

Saving a video to your computer is useful for editing, presenting offline, archiving your own content or watching on a slow connection. This guide shows how to download videos, whole playlists and audio on a Windows PC in the quality you want, using Zenra Video Downloader.

Download responsibly. Only download content you own, have permission to use, or that the platform allows you to save, and follow each platform's terms.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC.
  • Zenra Video Downloader — every new PC gets a 1-day free trial with full access.
  • The link to the video, playlist or channel.

Step by step

  1. Install the app. Download it from the product page, open the installer and follow the steps. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway — new apps without a paid certificate show this once.
  2. Copy the link. Open the video in your browser or app and copy its address. It works with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Snapchat and many other sites.
  3. Paste it into the app. Paste one link, or several links at once for a batch.
  4. Choose the quality. Pick 4K, 1440p, 1080p or MP3 audio — or Best quality to get the highest version available.
  5. Download. Downloads run side by side, up to five at a time. When a file is ready, click Open to go straight to it.

Download a whole playlist or channel

Paste a playlist or channel link instead of a single video. The app saves every video into its own folder, so a course, a series or a creator's Shorts stay organized. You can pause the batch and resume it later.

Getting the best quality

  • Best quality automatically picks the highest resolution the video offers.
  • 4K needs a 4K source. If the original was uploaded in 1080p, that's the highest quality available.
  • Higher quality means bigger files. A few minutes of 4K video can take several hundred megabytes.
  • MP3 saves just the audio — handy for podcasts, lectures and music you have rights to.

Troubleshooting

  • A site stopped working. Websites change often. Use the one-click engine update in the app to get the latest fixes.
  • Downloads are slow. Run fewer downloads at the same time, or check your connection.
  • A video won't download. Private, members-only and paid videos can't be downloaded.
  • A download failed. Open your history and press Retry.

Download responsibly

Downloading is a convenience, not a license to reuse other people's work. Keep downloads for personal use unless you have permission, credit creators, and never re-upload content you don't own. If you're saving your own videos for editing — for example to turn a long video into short clips — you're in the clear.

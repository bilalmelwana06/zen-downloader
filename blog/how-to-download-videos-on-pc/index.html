How to Download Videos on PC in HD & 4K (Step-by-Step Guide 2026)

By Bilal Melwana • 25 September 2026

Saving a video to your computer should be simple. In this guide you will learn how to download videos on a Windows PC in HD or 4K using Zenra Video Downloader — no complicated settings, no browser extensions.

Important: only download videos you own or have permission to use, and respect each platform's terms and the creator's rights.

Step 1 — Install Zenra Video Downloader

Download the installer from the Zenra Video Downloader page, run it and follow the setup. You get a 1-day free trial automatically — no card and no sign-up.

Step 2 — Copy the video link

Open the video on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Snapchat or Pinterest and copy its link from the address bar or the platform's Share button.

Step 3 — Paste and choose quality

Click Paste in the app. Keep Best Quality to get the highest quality the video offers, or pick 4K, 1080p, 720p or MP3 audio.

Step 4 — Download

Press Download. You will see live progress, speed and time left. When it finishes, click Open to go straight to the file.

  • Many videos at once: paste several links (one per line). The app downloads them in parallel.

  • If something fails: press Retry on the card, or click Update Engine to get the latest download engine.
  • Instagram: some videos need you to be logged in — use the Connections tab once.

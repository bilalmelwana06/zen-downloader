Questions clients ask first

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly — we reply within one business day.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call we send a fixed quote in writing, split into milestones, so you know the full price before any work starts. The budget field on our contact form helps us suggest what fits your range.

How long does a project take?

As a guide: a landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a business website or online store 3–6 weeks, a pilot AI agent 2–3 weeks, a custom AI tool 4–12 weeks and a web application 6–16 weeks. Your proposal includes a timeline with dated milestones.

Do you work with clients in my country?

Yes. We work remotely with clients around the world, in English, and schedule calls around your time zone.

Who owns the work when it's finished?

You do. Once the project is paid, the code, designs and content are yours. Domains, hosting, stores and AI accounts are set up in your name from the start.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share confidential details.

How do we stay in touch during a project?

By email, WhatsApp or video call — whatever suits you — plus a short written update every week with a link to the latest working version.

How do payments work?

Usually a deposit to start and the rest by milestone. We agree on an international payment method that works for you, and the details are in your proposal.

What happens after launch?

We hand over every login, file and document, fix anything that isn't working as agreed, and offer optional monthly support, improvements and new features.

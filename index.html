Questions clients ask first

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly - we reply within one business day.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on your budget and on what you want to build. Start by filling in the project form and sharing your idea - a few lines are enough. We'll reply with a free estimate within one business day, and after a short chat you get a fixed quote in writing, split into milestones, before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

Not long. Most projects (a website, an online store, an AI tool or a first version of your app) are delivered within a few days. Bigger projects usually take about 5-6 days, sometimes a little more or less. We agree the exact timeline with you before we start, and it's written into your proposal.

Do you build mobile and desktop apps?

Yes. We build Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android apps - with installers, licensing, payments, automatic updates and the App Store and Google Play launch. See app development.

Do you work with clients in my country?

Yes. We work remotely with clients around the world, in English, and schedule calls around your time zone.

Who owns the work when it's finished?

You do. Once the project is paid, the code, designs and content are yours. Domains, hosting, stores and AI accounts are set up in your name from the start.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share confidential details.

How do we stay in touch during a project?

By email, WhatsApp or video call, whatever suits you, plus short progress updates and a link to the latest working version while we build.

How do payments work?

Usually a deposit to start and the rest by milestone. We agree on an international payment method that works for you, and the details are in your proposal.

What happens after launch?

We hand over every login, file and document, fix anything that isn't working as agreed, and offer optional monthly support, improvements and new features.

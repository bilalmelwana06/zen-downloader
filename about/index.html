About Zenra AI

We're an AI and software development studio. We build AI agents, custom AI tools, websites, web apps and online stores for businesses around the world — and our own apps for creators.

Most businesses don't need more software. They need the right software — built well, explained clearly and owned by them.

That's why Zenra AI exists. We start by understanding the problem, build only what helps, and make sure you own — and can run — everything we deliver.

We began by building products of our own: a video downloader, a video splitter and an AI app that turns long videos into short clips. Shipping software to real users — with licensing, payments, automatic updates and support — taught us what makes technology work outside a demo.

Today we bring the same discipline to client work. Strategy, design, development and support sit with one team, so you get one plan, one point of contact and people who care whether the project works long after launch.

What we believe

Six principles that shape every project, from a landing page to an AI system.

  • Build only what helps

    We'll tell you when something isn't worth building — even if it means a smaller project for us.

  • Show, don't tell

    Working software every week beats long reports. You judge progress with your own eyes.

  • Honest by default

    Fixed quotes, realistic timelines and straight answers — including the ones you'd rather not hear.

  • Your business, your ownership

    Code, accounts and data belong to you from day one. No lock-in.

  • Security is not optional

    Careful access, no shared passwords and safe defaults on every project.

  • Always learning

    AI moves fast. We test new tools on our own products first, so you get what works — not what's hyped.

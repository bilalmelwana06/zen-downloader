Five steps, no surprises

Every project follows the same path, whatever its size.

  1. Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call about your goals, users, timeline and budget. No obligation.

    Notes and a recommended next step

  2. Proposal & fixed quote

  4. Build fast, with live previews

Ways to work together

Fixed-price projects for defined builds, and monthly options for ongoing work.

Monthly retainer

Clear pricing, paid in milestones

You know the full price before we start, and you pay as approved work is delivered.

  • A fixed price for the agreed scope
  • Usually a deposit to start, then payment by milestone
  • Changes outside the scope are quoted before we do them
  • International payment options agreed in your proposal
  • Hosting, platform subscriptions and AI usage paid by you directly — never marked up

Quality and security

The habits that keep your project safe — built into how we work, not added at the end.

  • Version control

    All code lives in Git, in a repository you own, with a clear history of every change.

  • Testing before release

    Features are tested on a preview or staging version before anything reaches your customers.

  • Least-privilege access

    We ask only for the access we need, and you can remove it at any time.

  • No shared passwords

    Accounts are created in your name; secrets stay in secure settings, never in code.

  • Backups

    Backups and a rollback plan for websites, stores and databases we run.

  • NDA on request

    We're happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share confidential details.

Everything is yours

When the project is paid, you own the work — and you can run it without us.

  • Source code in your own repository
  • Domains, hosting, stores and AI accounts in your name from day one
  • Design files and the content we created
  • Documentation and a recorded or live handover session
  • Admin access transferred — and our access removed whenever you ask

Questions about working with us

Can't find your answer? Send us your question — we reply within one business day.

Do I need a detailed brief?

No. A few sentences, a budget range and a couple of examples you like are enough. The discovery call fills in the rest, and we write the detailed scope for you to approve.

Can we change the scope during the project?

Yes. Small adjustments are part of normal work. Bigger changes are quoted in writing before we do them, so the price never changes by surprise.

What do you need from us?

A main contact who can make decisions, quick feedback on the previews we share, and the content and access listed in your proposal. We tell you exactly what we need and when.

How fast do you deliver?

Fast. Most projects are delivered within a few days, and bigger projects usually take about 5–6 days — sometimes a little more or less. We agree the exact timeline with you before we start, and it's written into your proposal.

Which time zone do you work in?

We work remotely with clients worldwide and plan calls around your time zone. Most communication is written, so you always have a record of decisions.

What if we're not happy with the work?

You see progress as we build, so problems surface early and are fixed early. Payments follow approved milestones, and we'll always tell you plainly if something isn't working.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We fix anything that doesn't work as agreed, and you can choose a monthly support, maintenance or improvement plan if you want us to stay on.

Share a few details about your project. We reply within one business day with questions or next steps — no obligation.

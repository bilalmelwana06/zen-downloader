Common questions

About custom web development. Can't find your answer? Send us your question.

How much does a custom website or web app cost?

It depends on the number of pages or features, the integrations and who writes the content. A landing page is a much smaller project than a web app with accounts and payments. After a short call you receive a fixed quote split into milestones.

How long does it take?

About 1–2 weeks for a landing page, 3–6 weeks for a business website and 6–16 weeks for a web application, depending on scope. Your proposal includes dated milestones.

What's the difference between a website and a web app?

A website mainly presents information and brings inquiries. A web app is software people log in to and use — a client portal, a booking system, a dashboard or a SaaS product. Many businesses start with a website and add app features later.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We set up easy editing for the pages and posts you change often, and show you how to use it in a short handover session.

Will my website rank on Google?

We build every site on solid SEO foundations: speed, structure, metadata and schema. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so we can help with ongoing SEO after launch — and we never promise a #1 position.

Can you connect it to our CRM, payments or booking system?

Yes. We integrate the tools you already use — CRMs, payment providers, booking, email and analytics — through their APIs.

Can you redesign my site without losing rankings?

Yes. We map every old URL to the new one, keep or improve titles and metadata, and set up redirects so search engines and visitors land in the right place.

Who owns the website and the code?

You do. The code lives in your repository, and the domain and hosting are in your name — often on a free or low-cost plan.

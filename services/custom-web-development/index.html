Is this for you?

Custom web development means designing and coding a website or web app around your business instead of forcing it into a template, so it loads fast and grows with you. It is a good fit when one of these sounds familiar.

  • Your website looks dated or loads slowly on phones
  • You need a portal, dashboard or web app, not just pages
  • Visitors leave without contacting you
  • Your team can't update the site without a developer

Common questions

Answers about custom web development. Can't find yours? Send us your question or read our guide on desktop apps vs web apps.

How much does a custom website or web app cost?

It depends on your budget and on what you need, such as the pages or features, the integrations and who writes the content. Start by filling in the project form and sharing your idea, even if it's rough. We'll reply with a free estimate, and after a short chat you get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Fast. Landing pages and business websites are usually delivered within a few days. Bigger web apps with accounts, payments or dashboards usually take about 5 to 6 days, sometimes a little more or less, depending on scope. Your proposal includes the agreed delivery date.

What's the difference between a website and a web app?

A website mainly presents information and brings inquiries. A web app is software people log in to and use, such as a client portal, a booking system, a dashboard or a SaaS product. Many businesses start with a website and add app features later.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We set up easy editing for the pages and posts you change often, and show you how to use it in a short handover session.

Will my website rank on Google?

We build every site on solid SEO foundations, including speed, structure, metadata and schema. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so we can help with ongoing SEO after launch, and we never promise a top ranking.

Can you connect it to our CRM, payments or booking system?

Yes. We integrate the tools you already use (CRMs, payment providers, booking, email and analytics) through their APIs.

Can you redesign my site without losing rankings?

Yes. We map every old URL to the new one, keep or improve titles and metadata, and set up redirects so search engines and visitors land in the right place.

Who owns the website and the code?

You do. The code lives in your repository, and the domain and hosting are in your name, often on a free or low-cost plan.

Will my website show up in ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI answers?

We build every site so AI assistants can read and cite it, with clear answers, structured data, fast pages, a sitemap and an llms.txt file. Whether an assistant mentions you also depends on your content and your reputation across the web, so we never promise a specific result.

How we approach AI projects

AI is only useful when it fits the work. These rules keep projects practical, measurable and affordable.

  • Start with the workflow

    We map the task, measure how long it takes today and decide where AI genuinely helps - and where plain software is better.

  • Prove it on your data

    A small working prototype on real examples comes before the full build, so you can judge quality early.

  • Choose the right model

    We compare OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and open-source models on quality, speed, cost and privacy - and keep them swappable.

  • Keep people in control

    Confidence checks, review steps and clear logs for anything that affects your customers or your money.

  • Keep running costs predictable

    Caching, batching and smart model routing keep the cost per task low and easy to forecast.

  • Protect your data

    Least-privilege access, encrypted secrets and providers that don't train on your data - or models on your own servers.

Common questions

About custom AI tool development. Can't find your answer? Send us your question. Guide: What is AI tool development?

How much does a custom AI tool cost?

It depends on your budget and on what the tool needs to do - a focused assistant is a much smaller project than a system connected to several apps. Start by filling in the project form and sharing your idea, even if it's rough. We'll reply with a free estimate, and after a short chat you get a fixed quote before any work starts. We can also start with a small prototype, so you see the value before the full build.

How long does it take?

Fast. A prototype is usually ready within a few days, and a production-ready tool in about 5-6 days, depending on how many systems it connects to. The exact timeline is agreed with you before we start and written into your proposal.

What's the difference between an AI tool and an AI agent?

An AI tool does one job when you use it - like extracting data from an invoice or answering a question from your documents. An AI agent works through multi-step tasks and takes actions across your apps on its own. Many projects start with a tool and grow into an agent.

Is our data safe?

We use your data only for your project and keep access to the minimum. We can use AI providers that don't train on API data, or run models on your own servers or devices when privacy requires it.

Which AI model will you use?

Whichever fits the task best. We compare quality, speed, cost and privacy, and design the tool so the model can be changed later without a rebuild.

Can you add AI to our existing software?

Yes. We add AI features through an API or a small service that sits next to your system, without rewriting what already works.

Who pays for the AI usage?

You do, directly. AI accounts are set up in your name, so you control usage and billing. We show you the expected running costs before launch.

Can the tool run offline or on our own servers?

Often, yes. Open-source models can run on your own servers or even on a user's PC - our own Viral Clips app does much of its work offline.

Can you build an AI chatbot trained on our documents?

Yes. We build AI chatbots and assistants that answer from your own documents, website and policies, show where each answer came from, and hand the conversation to a person when they're unsure. It can run on your website, WhatsApp or inside your own app.

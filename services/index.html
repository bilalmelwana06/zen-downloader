Questions about our services

Can't find your answer? Send us your question — we reply within one business day.

Can you handle more than one service for us?

Yes — that's how many clients work with us. A website, a store and an AI agent can be planned together, with one team and one point of contact.

Do you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We regularly add features to existing websites, stores and apps, connect tools you already use, and take over projects built by someone else.

Can we start small?

Yes. A landing page, an AI prototype or a pilot agent is a good first step. You see how we work before committing to anything bigger.

How do you price projects?

Fixed-scope work gets a fixed quote, paid by milestone. Ongoing work — like store management or agent care — has a fixed monthly fee agreed in advance.

Do you work with clients outside your time zone?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide, communicate in writing every week and schedule calls around your time zone.

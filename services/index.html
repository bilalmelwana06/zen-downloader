Questions about our services

Can't find your answer? Send us your question - we reply within one business day.

Can you handle more than one service for us?

Yes - that's how many clients work with us. A website, a store and an AI agent can be planned together, with one team and one point of contact.

Do you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We regularly add features to existing websites, stores and apps, connect tools you already use, and take over projects built by someone else.

Can we start small?

Yes. A landing page, an AI prototype, a pilot agent or a first version of your app is a good first step. You see how we work before committing to anything bigger.

How do you price projects?

Every project starts with a free estimate. Share your idea and your budget through the project form, and after a short chat we send a fixed quote, paid by milestone. Ongoing work, like store management or agent care, has a monthly fee agreed in advance.

How fast can you deliver?

Most projects are delivered within a few days. Bigger builds usually take about 5-6 days, sometimes a little more or less, and we agree the exact timeline with you before we start.

Do you work with clients outside your time zone?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide, send written progress updates while we build and schedule calls around your time zone.

Tell us what you're building.

Share a few details about your project. We reply within one business day with questions or next steps - no obligation.

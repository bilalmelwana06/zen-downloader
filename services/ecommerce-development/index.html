Common questions

About e-commerce store development. Can't find your answer? Send us your question.

Shopify or WooCommerce — which is better for me?

Shopify is the easiest to run and scales smoothly — ideal for most brands. WooCommerce gives more control and can cost less to run if you're comfortable with WordPress. We recommend one in your proposal based on your products, budget and team.

How much does an online store cost?

It depends on the number of products, the design and the integrations. After a short call you get a fixed quote split into milestones. Platform and app subscriptions are paid directly by you.

How long does it take?

A new store usually takes 3–6 weeks, and a migration 2–6 weeks, depending on catalog size and integrations.

Can you migrate my store without losing SEO?

Yes. We move products, customers and orders, keep URLs or redirect them, and carry over titles and descriptions so your rankings are protected.

Who owns the store?

You do. The store, domain and payment accounts are created in your name from day one.

Which payment methods can I accept?

Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay are available in most countries, plus local methods depending on where you sell. We confirm what's available for your business before we start.

What does monthly store management include?

Product updates, promotions, app and theme updates, speed and checkout checks, fixes and a monthly report. The exact scope and price are agreed in advance.

Can you add AI to my store?

Yes. A shopping assistant can answer product questions from your catalog, and an AI agent can handle order-status and return questions on your store and WhatsApp, handing anything unusual to your team.

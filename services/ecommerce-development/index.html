Shopify or WooCommerce?

Both run millions of stores. Here's how they differ - we'll recommend the one that fits your products, budget and team.

ComparedShopifyWooCommerce
What it isAn all-in-one, hosted store platformA free plugin that turns a WordPress site into a store
Hosting and securityIncluded and handled by ShopifyYour own hosting, kept updated by you or your developer
Running costsA monthly Shopify plan, plus paid apps and payment feesHosting and domain, plus paid extensions and payment fees
Ease of useThe easiest to run day to dayMore flexible, with a little more upkeep
Best forMost brands that want to start selling fastBrands that want full control or already use WordPress

Common questions

About e-commerce store development. Can't find your answer? Send us your question. Guide: Shopify vs WooCommerce

Shopify or WooCommerce - which is better for me?

Shopify is the easiest to run and scales smoothly - ideal for most brands. WooCommerce gives more control and can cost less to run if you're comfortable with WordPress. We recommend one in your proposal based on your products, budget and team.

How much does an online store cost?

It depends on your budget and on your store - the number of products, the design and the integrations. Start by filling in the project form and sharing your idea, even if it's rough. We'll reply with a free estimate, and after a short chat you get a fixed quote before any work starts. Platform and app subscriptions are paid by you directly.

How long does it take?

Fast. A new store is usually ready within a few days. Bigger stores and migrations usually take about 5-6 days, depending on catalog size and integrations. We agree the exact timeline with you before we start.

Can you migrate my store without losing SEO?

Yes. We move products, customers and orders, keep URLs or redirect them, and carry over titles and descriptions so your rankings are protected.

Who owns the store?

You do. The store, domain and payment accounts are created in your name from day one.

Which payment methods can I accept?

Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay are available in most countries, plus local methods depending on where you sell. We confirm what's available for your business before we start.

What does monthly store management include?

Product updates, promotions, app and theme updates, speed and checkout checks, fixes and a monthly report. The exact scope and price are agreed in advance.

Can you add AI to my store?

Yes. A shopping assistant can answer product questions from your catalog, and an AI agent can handle order-status and return questions on your store and WhatsApp, handing anything unusual to your team.
